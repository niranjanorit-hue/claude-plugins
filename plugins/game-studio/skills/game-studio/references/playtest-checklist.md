# Playtest checklist

- [ ] A new player understands the goal within 10 seconds without reading text.
- [ ] Controls respond on the same frame as input.
- [ ] Losing feels fair: the player can see why they failed.
- [ ] Restart takes one key or tap and under one second.
- [ ] Runs at a steady 60 fps on desktop and stays playable on a phone.
- [ ] Works with the tab resized, and pauses when the tab loses focus.
- [ ] No console errors during a full play session.
- [ ] Sound can be muted, and the game is playable with sound off.
